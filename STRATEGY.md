# Strategy brief: week of 16–22 Nov 2026

Sun 15 Nov 2026, Growth Strategist. Daily rhythm (PUSH + TEST, 3+ pitch posts a week) unchanged.

## This week's angle

**One bad day ends the account.** A loss, a "make it back" trade, a bigger one, and the daily loss limit closes the evaluation. The Wharf shows today's loss against the daily limit on each prop account, before the next trade.

## Who it's for (job + awareness level)

- **Job:** "When I'm on an evaluation, I want to see how much room I have left today before I trade, so one bad morning doesn't end the account."
- **Awareness:** problem aware. Pitch posts: solution aware.

## Hooks to use

1. "Day 27 of 30. One bad morning."
2. "Guess: how many losses until your daily limit?"
3. "4 losses. That's all you get today." (tag "Example: $250 risk, $1,000 daily limit")
4. "Lost the first trade? Don't take the next."
5. "Breached for no reason? Check this number."

## Formats to carry it

Dramatised story with a deadline ("30 days to pass"), Guess before the reveal, Toxic vs Normal pour test, Dave under 15s. Readable premise in frame 1.

## Post asks

1. **PUSH: dramatised story, ~18s.** Hook 1 → loss, "I'll make it back" → two more, bigger → "Daily loss limit hit. Account closed." → rewind → the real "Today vs daily limit" meter (generic account name, no firm preset) → she stops → "Still trading tomorrow." "Dramatisation" throughout, "Sample data shown" on app screens. Comment: ROOM. **Measure:** IG avg watch and 3s rate vs 30-day medians.
2. **TEST: Guess before the reveal, 11–14s.** Hook 2, corner tag "Example: $25,000 account · 1% risk · $1,000 daily loss limit". Dave guesses 10 → the app's onboarding line "That's $250 a trade, about 4 full losses before your $1,000 daily limit" → "4. Not 10." → "Already down $400? 2 left." → CTA to the free position size calculator. **Measure:** IG saves + shares per 1,000 views vs last week; comments.

## Call to action

"See your daily-loss room before you trade. Free plan at thewharf.app" (1 prop account). Sizing posts: "Work out yours free: thewharf.app/position-size-calculator".

## Avoid

- **No journal price and no "about US$62–119" total this week** (a PITCH.md price is being re-checked). Pitch posts use "One subscription instead of three or four" or "Less than one journal subscription, and the journal is only part of it".
- Promising a pass, payout or funding.
- Claiming the app tracks open P&L unless the screen shows it.
- Any hint at a firm, platform or competitor; other tools' Black Friday sales; countdowns.
- Dave over 15s, loops without a readable premise, comment-vote endings.
- AI trade reviews and the AI Trading Agent.
