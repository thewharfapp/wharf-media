# Strategy brief: week of 23–29 Nov 2026

Sun 22 Nov 2026, Growth Strategist. Daily rhythm (PUSH + TEST, 3+ pitch posts a week) unchanged.

## This week's angle

**A green day lifts the floor.** On trailing drawdown, a good day moves the floor up, so the next red day has less room than it feels like. The Wharf tracks trailing drawdown, daily loss and consistency per prop account: you see today's room before the next trade.

## Who it's for (job + awareness level)

- **Job:** "When I'm on an evaluation or funded account, I want to see how much room I really have left after a green day, so one red day doesn't end the account."
- **Awareness:** problem aware. Pitch posts: solution aware.

## Hooks to use

1. "A green day lifts the floor."
2. "Up $800. Room left: still $2,000." (tag "Example: $50K account, $2,000 trailing drawdown")
3. "Just over $250 in drawdown left. Now what?" (tag "Example")
4. "Your big day pushed your payout further away." (consistency rules; add "check your own rules")
5. "Does your drawdown floor follow you up?"

Worked example (label "Example"): $50K account, $2,000 end-of-day trail. +$800 → floor $48,800, room still $2,000. −$1,000 next day → room $1,000.

## Avoid

- Prices, bills, the "three subscriptions" total and the "category list → keep The Wharf" device (worn out). Black Friday sales, countdowns.
- One drawdown rule as universal: trailing differs (end-of-day vs intraday, where it stops). Say "check your own rules".
- Any number without "Example" or "Sample data shown". No pass, payout or funding promises.
- Naming, showing or hinting at a prop firm, its platform, a broker or another app (including look-alike tier names or UI).
- AI trade reviews and the AI Trading Agent.
