## Rules for using the pitch

- Use only the numbers on this page. Call them "typical" or say "up to". Put US$ on theirs and A$ on ours, and don't convert currencies.
- Safe comparisons: "less than one journal subscription", "one subscription instead of three or four", "about US$62–119 a month for all three". Don't say "cheaper than any of them": a basic charting plan can cost less than The Wharf on its own.
- Never name, show or hint at a specific brand, logo or screenshot of another product, and never show a fake bill or receipt from one. A generic "subscriptions" list with category names and the typical prices above is fine. Mark it "Typical prices, Nov 2026".
- Don't claim The Wharf fully replaces any one product. Say it covers the same jobs: journal, charts, replay, backtesting and prop rules.
- Data: crypto charts are live; gold, forex and stocks refresh every few minutes, not tick by tick. Don't say "real-time data on every market".
- Charts, replay and strategy search are Pro. If a post says "free", it means the free plan with the limits above.
- Don't mention AI trade reviews until Will confirms the feature is live.
- No profit, income or funding promises. App screens show "Sample data shown".
